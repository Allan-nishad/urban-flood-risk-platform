# Phase 5.2 — Master Execution Summary & Reconciliation Closure

## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 5.2 (**Scientific Reconciliation & Evidence-Integrity Closure**) of the **AI-Based Early Detection and Forecasting of Urban Flood Risks Using Satellite and Weather Data** platform (`urban-flood-risk-platform`) has completed.

Governed by the non-negotiable principle that **source authenticity takes absolute priority over numerical performance**, this campaign performed an exhaustive, read-only-first scientific audit across all platform components.

### Core Reconciled Findings
1. **Forecast Provenance:** The 198,900 forecast rows in `archived_nwp_forecasts_bengaluru.parquet` were generated by an internal mathematical reconstruction model calibrated to historical observed rainfall event dates. They were not downloaded from genuine ECMWF, GFS, or IMD NWP archives. Mandatory classification: **`OBSERVATION_CALIBRATED_RETROSPECTIVE_SERIES`**. Finding: **`GENUINE_NWP_FORECAST_VERIFICATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED`**.
2. **Degrees of Freedom & 711:** The value 711 represents the count of distinct `(source_pixel_id, valid_date, lead_time_hours, accumulation_interval_hours)` tuples (`N_anchor_time_lead_groups`). It is **NOT** a statistically established effective sample size. True statistical degrees of freedom remain **`NOT_ESTABLISHED`** due to spatial covariance, temporal autocorrelation, and shared retrospective calibration curves.
3. **Metric Equivalence & Aggregation Inflation:** Raw cell-level errors ($\text{MAE} = 5.973, 11.503, 20.805\text{ mm}$) are 100% identical between initial Phase 5 and Phase 5 Final. Reported correlation increases ($0.72 \to 0.86$ at 6h) resulted purely from averaging across cells within anchors (aggregation noise reduction), not from improved data accuracy.
4. **Flood Evidence & High-Ground Negative Controls:** 98.13% of the study area ($1,148$ of $1,170$ cells) is unobserved and censored. The 5 negative control sites are elevated AWS/CPCB weather stations on topographic ridges ($>888\text{ m}$) lacking flood depth sensors (**`CONDITIONALLY_MONITORED_HIGH_GROUND_PROXIES`**). Zero verified dry valley ground-truth observations exist. The 95% bootstrap CI for ROC-AUC spans $[0.294, 1.000]$ ($p = 0.0867$). Gate B is formally **`BLOCKED_BY_DATA_ACCESS`** for supervised flood prediction.
5. **Authentic SAR Status:** Only CDSE catalogue search metadata exists on disk. No Level-1 GRD SAFE packages or SNAP processing logs exist. Status remains **`SCENE_AUDIT_ONLY`**. Synthetic fixture remains strictly quarantined.
6. **Platform Integrity:** All 49 historical datasets across the three previous manifests match their recorded SHA-256 hashes ($100\%$). All 94 automated regression tests across 14 test files pass ($100\%$).
